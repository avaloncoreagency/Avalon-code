# Sales playbook for Miguel and Samuel

Source of truth: existing CRM 13-stage pipeline, Discovery, Deal Review, signed contract gate and Sales Core. These notes do not replace the CRM.

## Workflow
1. Capture a consented or otherwise permitted inquiry and check potential duplicates.
2. Discovery: industry, business needs, current workflow, service coverage, capacity, decision maker, budget and timing.
3. Diagnose based on documented facts; unknown remains unknown. Risk and capability separate from lead score.
4. Prepare a versioned proposal with scope, exclusion, pricing, taxes, provider fees, delivery criteria and term.
5. Confirm the owner-authorized quote and legal contract. No Won status without signed, verified contract.
6. Onboard through delegated access, verified content, responsible people, dates, privacy, success criteria and issue tracking.
7. Deliver, test and obtain acceptance. Support and change orders follow the contract.

## Industry questions
- Contractors: average ticket, known margin, ZIP coverage, handling capacity, licensing, callback speed and commission terms.
- Appointment businesses: staff availability, booking rules, deposits/cancellations and expected appointment volume.
- Retail: variants, inventory, payment processor, delivery, refunds, tax responsibilities and product content.

## Standard language
Opening: 'Antes de recomendarte un servicio, me gustaría comprender cómo funciona tu negocio y qué necesitas mejorar.'
Quote explanation: 'Implementación, mensualidad, costos externos y exclusiones se muestran por separado.'
Refund/dispute: 'Registraremos la discrepancia y revisaremos las evidencias y el contrato antes de ajustar importes.'

## Release constraint
These are internal human playbooks. Do not auto-email prospects, generate a contract, create a sale or write client notes from the private guidance page.
