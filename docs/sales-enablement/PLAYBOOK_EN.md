## 1. What Avalon stands for
Avalon Core Agency develops digital solutions for businesses: websites, online stores and custom CRM systems. Our approach is consultative: understand how the business works, preserve useful existing tools and define a scope we can actually deliver.

**Suggested positioning, not a promise:** “We help local businesses present their services more clearly and evaluate digital systems that fit their operations.” Never guarantee leads, revenue, search rankings or ROI. Never invent testimonials, past clients or case studies.

Use the company name **Avalon Core Agency** and the owners' human names **Miguel Hobaica** and **Samuel Muñoz**. Technical account identifiers are not names. Sales agents never impersonate owners, sign contracts or negotiate unapproved discounts.

## 2. What we can discuss vs. what we can sell
The integrated STABLE V1 report marks the three core service families **NOT SELLABLE** until business, financial, legal, QA and owner approvals. A feature visible inside Avalon OS is not automatically a service we can contract.

## 3. Ideal prospects and disqualification
- **Roofing/fencing:** local contractors with a confirmed need to clarify services, service area, materials or legitimately licensed project imagery. Do not assume licensing or a conversion problem.
- **Barbershops:** service list, hours, team and existing booking link; do not replace tools merely for convenience.
- **HVAC/trades:** coverage, emergency channels and actual availability; never claim 24/7 unless verified.
- **Restaurants:** menu, hours and existing ordering/booking links; not a new checkout.
- **Dental practices:** institutional information only, subject to professional/privacy review; no patient history in discovery.

Disqualify when the need depends on a blocked product, the existing site already works, permission is absent, the buyer refuses contact, essential rights are missing or they insist on unsupported guarantees.

## 4. The human-led selling journey
| Stage | Authorized seller's responsibility | Evidence | STOP condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research | Public URL, date, observable fact and hypothesis | Source record | Scraping or unsupported assumptions |
| Contact review | Duplicates, allowed channel, legal review, opt-out and owner approval | Approved contact basis | No permission or do-not-contact |
| First approach | Ask relevant questions; do not pitch unapproved services | Actual date/channel/result | Refusal or disallowed channel |
| Qualification | Apply the evidence-based 1–5 score | Reason and date | Insufficient facts |
| Discovery | Need, existing tools, decision-maker and priorities | Minimal authorized notes | Sensitive data or false promises |
| Demo | Show only verified/approved version; label concept as concept | QA-approved demonstration evidence | Unverified product shown as live |
| Quote | Approved SKU, pricing, scope, exclusions and authority | Version + confirmed sending | N/D costs or blocked offering |
| Negotiation | Clarify objections within approved terms | Approved changes | Unauthorized concessions |
| Close | Validate contract and transaction flow | Signed/verified agreement | Verbal yes only |
| Follow-up | Follow legally approved cadence or mutually agreed next action | Actual event | Opt-out, stop condition |
| Operations handoff | Pass the minimum approved record to delivery owner | Explicit acceptance | Missing contract/permissions |

Remember: prospect ≠ customer; scheduled ≠ held meeting; quote draft ≠ sent quote; score 5 ≠ won; contract ≠ invoice; invoiced ≠ paid.

## 5. Manual lead qualification: 1–5
**1:** no fit or rejection; close respectfully and record an opt-out if given.
**2:** potential fit, unconfirmed need; research, do not propose.
**3:** buyer states a need, but key details are missing; discover.
**4:** need, decision-maker, priority and an **approved product** match; validate scope.
**5:** decision-maker requests proposal; SKU, scope and conditions are approved; prepare approved quote, not a won sale.

No supporting information = **UNQUALIFIED / N/A**, not an invented score. Hunter's 0–100 score measures completeness; do not convert or overwrite it. **DO NOT CONTACT** is a separate constraint. WP-01 is not sellable today, so do not qualify it 4/5 for the purpose of quoting.

## 6. Discovery questions
1. Which services and locations should people understand quickly?
2. How do customers currently find you and make inquiries?
3. Which existing tools must be preserved?
4. What specific problem would you improve, in your own words?
5. Who approves changes and signs?
6. What priority or timing is important, if you're comfortable sharing?
7. Who owns the text, images, domain and accounts?
8. Are there privacy/professional requirements that exclude certain data?
9. How would you evaluate a proposal if an approved solution becomes available?
10. Which next contact channel and date, if any, would you consent to?

For channel-ready drafts and sector questions, use [bilingual scripts](SCRIPTS_ES_EN.md) and [roofing/fencing kit](../pm-v3/sales/sector-outreach-kit.md).

## 7. Demos and quotes
An actual demo must identify product, version, environment and validated capabilities. Never present a storyboard or fictional example as a deployed client project. A screenshot from another business is not an Avalon case study.

Do not quote before confirming an authorized SKU, real cost/pricing approval, scope and exclusions, third-party costs, ownership of accounts, the legal contract, seller's authority, Operations' delivery capacity and permission to send. **N/A is a blocker, not $0.** A versioned quote must have a verified sent date and a private reference. Changes require another approval; the salesperson cannot modify contract terms or pricing unilaterally.

## 8. Negotiation, trust and objections
Use **question → evidence → authorized options → buyer choice**.
- “I already have a site”: “Great. Let's first see whether a change would add measurable value.”
- “Too expensive”: “We can compare scope and included costs, but I cannot offer an unapproved discount.”
- “Can you guarantee customers?”: “No; we can specify verifiable deliverables, not guarantee your sales.”
- “Commission only”: “There is no standard WP-01 commission offer. Exceptions need Finance, Legal and owner approval.”
- “Why trust Avalon?”: “We'll outline real deliverables, responsibilities, approval criteria and contract terms. We won't claim undocumented results.”
- “Not interested”: “Understood. Thank you; we will respect your preference.”

## 9. Follow-up and closing
A **proposed only** outbound sequence, subject to channel/recipient approval: initial permitted contact, one relevant follow-up after three business days, a final follow-up after ten business days if still lawful, then stop. Respond to inbound only for its stated purpose; agree on any further contact. Opt-outs override every reminder.

A deal becomes **won** only through the authorized contractual/transaction gate, never because a salesperson labels it or receives an oral yes. Do not claim agents can create invoices, alter sales or view commissions until permissions and functionality are verified.

## 10. CRM and Agent Portal
The canonical CRM has **13 detailed stages and five visual groups**: New (new, researched, contact-pending); In conversation (contacted, replied, qualified, meeting, diagnosed, nurture); Proposal (proposal, negotiation); Won (won); Lost (lost).

Seller access depends on assigned roles and grants. The proposed staff portal and seller commercial views are under review in draft PR #349: **do not describe draft functionality as available in production**. Never sign in using an owner's credentials or try to bypass a denied permission.

When an authorized private workflow exists, log a verified business identity, public source, agency_b2b audience, assigned owner, true stage, confirmed need, explained 1–5 rating (in an approved private place), consent/channel or opt-out, actual interaction and next permitted action. No medical records, credentials or unnecessary personal data. A completed note is not proof of an external call. If access is missing, escalate; do not create parallel CRM records in spreadsheets or public docs.

## 11. Handoff to Client Operations
Share only through an authorized private channel: canonical opportunity/customer reference, verified authorized decision-maker, signed service/version, scope and exclusions, contractual/payment references (not payment card data), delivery owner, agreed milestones, content rights, domain/hosting ownership, tools to preserve, risks and next agreed action. Operations must acknowledge acceptance before delivery work begins. Avoid customer details in public GitHub documents.

## 12. Daily standards and final acceptance
Start with your own assigned leads, consent/opt-outs and due actions. During work, log only actual activities. End with a next action, deadline and blocker report to your supervisor. Do not invent quotas or commissions; see [daily operations](DAILY_OPERATIONS_ES_EN.md).
